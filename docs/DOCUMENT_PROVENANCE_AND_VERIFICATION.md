# CONVELINA — Document Provenance & Verification Classification Policy

## 1. Statutory Mandate & Purpose

In government document processing and identity resolution, conflating an official statutory circular with an application-generated template or a development sample introduces catastrophic legal risks for citizens.

CONVELINA enforces an uncompromising distinction between:
1. **Provenance Classification**: The custodial origin and production nature of the document asset.
2. **Verification State**: The statutory validity, currency, and legal applicability of the contents.

---

## 2. Seven Distinct Provenance Classifications

Every document cataloged in CONVELINA is assigned exactly one immutable or audit-controlled provenance classification:

| Provenance Classification | Definition & Criteria | Permissible Uses in CONVELINA |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `OFFICIAL_ORIGINAL` | Authentic, unedited digital artifact directly obtained from an official government gazette, ministry portal, or statutory body (e.g., UIDAI, ITD, MEA, EPFO, CBSE). Verified via publication metadata and cryptographic SHA-256 hash. | Statutory claims, official download, evidence checklists, primary procedural basis. |
| `OFFICIAL_COPY` | Certified copy or exact mirror of an official government notification hosted on secondary government repositories (e.g., state portals, high court digital libraries). | Supporting procedural guidance, checklist evidence. |
| `GOVERNMENT_REFERENCE` | Official government manual, FAQs, training material, or internal guideline (e.g., `DOC_043`). Contains official rules but is not a gazetted statutory enactment. | Procedural guidance with source limitation notice; requires human verification for exceptions. |
| `HISTORICAL_REFERENCE` | Official notification or circular that has been formally superseded, repealed, or sunsetted by newer legal enactments. | Audit lineage, historical cross-reference. **Forbidden as current procedural instruction.** |
| `APPLICATION_GENERATED` | Structured legal templates, representation drafts, or affidavit forms generated by CONVELINA's internal document generator (Module 7). | Citizen draft generation, preview, filing assistance. Must never be labeled as official government forms. |
| `SYNTHETIC_DEVELOPMENT_SAMPLE` | Mock PDF, test fixture, or ReportLab-generated document created during development, testing, or benchmarking. | Local E2E testing, CI/CD pipelines, UI demonstration. **Mandatory warning banner enforced.** |
| `UNVERIFIED_SOURCE` | Document ingested from third-party websites, citizen uploads, or unverified community archives lacking official ministerial confirmation. | Quarantined; never exposed as verified statutory law; flagged for administrative audit. |

---

## 3. Five Independent Verification States

Independent of custodial provenance, each document maintains a verification lifecycle state:

| Verification State | Operational Meaning | System Enforcement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `VERIFIED` | Reviewed, authenticated against official gazettes/APIs, and confirmed as active statutory law. | Accessible to citizens for definitive procedural guidance and checklist generation. |
| `NEEDS_REVIEW` | Recently ingested or metadata updated; pending administrative audit and evidentiary review. | Hidden from definitive citizen guidance; accessible only in exploratory admin repository. |
| `OUTDATED` | Newer statutory guidance exists, but formal deprecation circular is pending. | Highlighted with "Caution: May Be Outdated" banner; directs citizen to search recent circulars. |
| `RETIRED` | Formally rescinded, repealed, or replaced by statutory amendment. | Excluded from procedural synthesis; preserved in historical archive for auditability. |
| `SOURCE_METADATA_CONFLICT` | Retains contradictory dates, unconfirmed administrative claims, or conflicting jurisdictional authority (e.g., `DOC_043`). | Flagged with purple `Source Conflict Notice`; mandatory `human_review_required = True`; attached limitation notice. |

## 4. Mandatory Provenance Restrictions & Guardrails

### 4.1. The ReportLab & Application-Generated Invariant
> **Strict Prohibition**: A ReportLab-generated or application-generated PDF must **never** be labeled, described, or presented as an official government form.

Backend verification in `backend/app/routers/repository.py` actively validates document claims. If an administrator attempts to promote a synthetic or application-generated asset to `OFFICIAL_ORIGINAL` or `OFFICIAL_COPY`:
```python
if is_reportlab_or_synthetic(asset) and requested_provenance in ("OFFICIAL_ORIGINAL", "OFFICIAL_COPY"):
    raise HTTPException(
        status_code=400,
        detail="Cannot classify application-generated or synthetic ReportLab asset as OFFICIAL_ORIGINAL."
    )
```

### 4.2. Synthetic Sample Disclaimer Enforcement
Whenever a document with `provenance = SYNTHETIC_DEVELOPMENT_SAMPLE` or `is_synthetic_sample = True` is rendered in the UI or serialized via the API, the following banner is mandatory:
> `SYNTHETIC DEVELOPMENT SAMPLE — NOT AN OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT FORM`
> *"This document is an application-generated template constructed for development and testing. Download authentic original forms directly from the issuing authority portal."*

### 4.3. Preserving Cryptographic SHA-256 Integrity
Every official resource records its exact SHA-256 digest upon ingestion. Administrators cannot alter source files in place or erase cryptographic hashes. Any revision creates a new audited version.

---

## 5. Administrative Verification Workflow & Audit Trails

To prevent administrative privilege escalation or silent tampering:
1. **Evidentiary Requirement**: Moving a document to `VERIFIED` requires explicit submission of evidentiary grounds (e.g., gazette notification reference, ministerial URL, and verifier remarks).
2. **Two System Roles Only**: Centralized authorization enforces strictly two roles: `USER` and `ADMIN`. No intermediate or unvetted "officer" roles exist.
3. **Immutable Audit Logging**: Every verification approval and lifecycle transition triggers an audit event via `AuditService.log_event` recorded in the cryptographic, append-only `audit_log` table.
